Introduction to AI Marketing Expert (AIME)

AI Marketing Expert (v1.2.7) transforms your WordPress website into a self-driving, AI-powered marketing headquarters. Instead of spending thousands of dollars every year on disparate SaaS subscriptions (such as Mailchimp, Apollo, Zapier, Jasper, CartBounty, Semrush, or Intercom), you get an all-in-one ecosystem that runs directly inside your WordPress dashboard with zero monthly per-contact penalties.

Autonomous Cold Outreach

Find verified B2B leads by job title and industry, validate DNS MX deliverability, and enroll them into cold email nurture funnels.

Visual Automation Canvas

Build branching multi-step workflows with drag-and-drop nodes, delay timers (hours/days), or generate full graphs with natural language prompts.

WooCommerce Cart Recovery

Capture guest shopper emails live during checkout before order submission and recover lost revenue with 1-click single-item & single-qty deep links.

Key Advantages & Architecture

  • Zero Tool Switching: Email campaigns, SEO audits, blog creation, chatbots, and social posts all live inside your WordPress admin.
  • Your AI, Your Keys: Connect Google AI Studio (Free Gemini tier), OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, or OpenRouter with AES-256-GCM encryption.
  • No Monthly Per-Subscriber Penalties: Unlike Mailchimp or Klaviyo, the free plan includes unlimited email contacts.
  • E-Commerce Revenue Multiplier: Automate WooCommerce post-purchase review funnels, re-engagement offers, and cart recovery.

Free Tier vs Pro License

  • Free Tier (Lifetime Free): Unlimited contacts, 30 campaigns/month, 2 active automations, 20 generated articles/month, 10 keyword searches, 1 chatbot, real-time cart tracking.
  • Pro License (Power Users): Unlimited campaigns, unlimited visual workflows, B2B Autopilot pipeline, Rank Tracking, Knowledge Base URL/WooCommerce scraping, Human Chatbot Takeover, dynamic coupons, and A/B split testing.

Installation & Setup Guide

System Requirements

  • WordPress: 6.2 or higher (tested up to WP 7.x).
  • PHP: 8.0 or higher (PHP 8.2 or 8.3 recommended for optimal performance).
  • MySQL / MariaDB: MySQL 5.7+ or MariaDB 10.3+.
  • PHP Extensions: curl, openssl, mbstring, json.
  • Optional (E-commerce): WooCommerce 7.0+ for abandoned cart recovery and product knowledge sync.

Step-by-Step Installation

  1. Log in to your WordPress Admin dashboard and navigate to Plugins → Add New.
  2. Click Upload Plugin at the top and select the ai-marketing-expert.zip file.
  3. Click Install Now and then click Activate Plugin.
  4. A new top-level menu item named AI Marketing will appear in your WordPress sidebar.
  5. If you have purchased a Pro license, enter and verify your key under Settings → License.

Security Notice: AES-256-GCM Encryption at Rest

All sensitive credentials (AI provider API keys, SMTP passwords, OAuth access tokens) are automatically encrypted at rest in your MySQL database using AES-256-GCM with keys derived from your WordPress security salts (wp-config.php). No plain text secrets are ever logged or exposed.

Instead of locking you into a single expensive email provider, AIME allows you to configure multiple SMTP relays with automated round-robin rotation and failover. By stacking reputable free-tier providers, you can send thousands of cold and marketing emails every single day at near-zero cost:

Real-Time Webhook Callbacks & Bounce Hygiene

Maintaining a 99%+ deliverability score requires instant handling of bad addresses. AIME provides a dedicated Webhook Callback URL for your SMTP providers:

https://yourdomain.com/wp-json/aime/v1/smtp/callback?token=YOUR_SECRET_TOKEN
  • Instant Bounce Tracking: When SendGrid, Brevo, or Amazon SES detects a hard bounce (invalid mailbox), the callback triggers immediately. AIME marks the subscriber as BOUNCED, permanently halting future sends.

  • Unsubscribe Sync: One-click native unsubscribe headers (RFC 8058) are synchronized across all lists.

Warm-Up & Sending Throttling: For new sending domains, always configure the Sending Speed Throttler under Module Settings to no more than 20 emails every 5 minutes. Incrementally increase the batch size by 15% daily to build ironclad domain reputation.

External Server Cron Setup (Zero-Delay Email Dispatch)

WordPress default cron (wp-cron.php) only triggers when someone visits your website. On low-traffic sites, this can cause abandoned cart recovery emails or scheduled campaigns to fire hours late. Configuring an external server cron eliminates delays completely.

Canonical External Cron Webhook URL:

Use your site's dedicated cron webhook with your secret token to trigger execution from any server:

https://yourdomain.com/wp-json/aime/v1/cron/execute?secret_token=YOUR_CRON_SECRET

Method 1: cPanel / Server Crontab (Every 5 Minutes)

Under cPanel Cron Jobs, create an entry running every 5 minutes:

*/5 * * * * curl -s -k "https://yourdomain.com/wp-json/aime/v1/cron/execute?secret_token=YOUR_SECRET" >/dev/null 2>&1

Method 2: Free cron-job.org Service

  1. Create a free account at cron-job.org.
  2. Click Create Cronjob and paste your full webhook URL.
  3. Set execution schedule to Every 5 minutes and save.

Troubleshooting & Real-World Pitfalls

1. Cart Recovery Emails Not Sending

Cause: WP-Cron is sleeping, or an aggressive page caching plugin is caching checkout scripts.

Fix: Configure an external server cron (see above) and add /checkout/ and /cart/ to the "Never Cache" exclusion rules in WP Rocket or LiteSpeed Cache.

2. Emails Landing in the Spam Folder

Cause: Sending via standard unauthenticated PHP mail() or missing domain SPF/DKIM records.

Fix: Configure a dedicated SMTP provider under Email Marketing → SMTP (Gmail App Password, Brevo, or SendGrid) and verify your domain's SPF, DKIM, and DMARC DNS records.

3. AI "Rate Limit Exceeded" Errors

Cause: Exceeding the 15 requests/minute quota on free Google AI Studio accounts during large batches.

Fix: In Settings → General, decrease background batch size to 5, or connect an OpenAI or OpenRouter fallback key.

4. Chatbot Widget Not Appearing

Cause: Theme script conflicts or JavaScript errors in the front-end console.

Fix: Verify the chatbot toggle is active under Chatbot → Settings, clear your browser and site cache, and ensure no other plugins are blocking REST API endpoints.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Troubleshooting

Authoritative answers to the most common setup, technical, and operational questions for AI Marketing Expert.

Pricing & Ownership

Are there any recurring monthly subscription fees?

No! AI Marketing Expert is 100% self-hosted on your own WordPress site. You never pay monthly per-subscriber penalties or database fees like Mailchimp or Klaviyo. If you connect Google AI Studio's free Gemini API tier, your AI copywriting and generation costs are also $0.

Email & Deliverability

Can I send marketing or cold outreach emails using Gmail, Amazon SES, or Brevo?

Yes! The plugin features a built-in Multi-SMTP Relay Manager supporting Gmail, Google Workspace, Amazon SES, Brevo (Sendinblue), SendGrid, Mailgun, and custom SMTP. You can configure daily sending limits and automatic failover rotation across multiple providers to protect your sender reputation.

Lead Generation

How does the B2B Lead Finder work, and are email addresses verified before sending?

The B2B Lead Finder identifies targeted prospects across roles, niches, and countries. Before any lead is enrolled into an email sequence, the built-in Email Validator performs real-time syntax checking, disposable email filtering, and live DNS MX record verification to ensure near 0% bounce rate.

WooCommerce Growth

How does WooCommerce Abandoned Cart Recovery recover lost sales?

When a shopper enters their details but abandons checkout, the plugin captures the cart. It automatically dispatches scheduled recovery emails containing a direct 1-click checkout recovery URL, complete with duplicate cart protection and optional dynamic discount incentives.

Performance

Will this plugin slow down my website or WooCommerce store?

No. The front-end tracking asset is ultra-lightweight (under 5KB) and loads asynchronously. All heavy operations such as AI generation, contact verification, and email dispatch run in background worker processes controlled by cron, preventing any impact on page load speed.

Visual Workflows

Can I build visual automated workflows and nurture drip sequences?

Yes! The visual Workflow Automation Canvas (React Flow) lets you build intuitive drag-and-drop automations with triggers (new order, abandoned cart, tag added), conditions, timed delays (e.g., wait 3 days), and automated actions (send email, webhook, tag update). You can also use Text-to-Workflow AI to generate flows automatically from plain English prompts.

AI SEO & Content

How does Deep Humanize in Content Generator bypass AI detectors?

Deep Humanize analyzes sentence rhythm, perplexity, and burstiness to rephrase synthetic AI patterns into authentic human tone. Crucially, unlike generic paraphrasers, it strictly preserves all Markdown tables, bullet lists, bold highlights, and technical terminology.

Conversational AI

Can the AI Chatbot answer customer questions using my WooCommerce products and site content?

Yes. The AI Chatbot includes a dedicated Knowledge Base crawler that indexes your WooCommerce product catalog, prices, and website pages. It delivers instant, context-aware answers to shopper questions and allows human agents to take over live conversations at any time.

Reliability

What should I do if WP-Cron fails or scheduled tasks are delayed?

For high-volume sites, you can disable standard WP-Cron and set up a reliable external server cron (via crontab or services like cron-job.org) pinging the canonical AIME webhook URL every 5 minutes for guaranteed task execution.

Migration

Can I migrate contacts from Mailchimp, FluentCRM, or CSV lists?

Yes. Export your contacts as a CSV file from any CRM and use Email Marketing → Import / Export. The visual column mapper lets you match name, email, custom fields, and assign tags in seconds.
