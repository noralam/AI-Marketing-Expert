## 🛠️ How to Load & Test Locally in Google Chrome:

1. Open **Google Chrome** and go to chrome://extensions/
2. Enable **Developer mode** (top right toggle).
3. Click **Load unpacked** (top left).
4. Select this directory:
   c:\laragon\www\tools\wp-content\plugins\ai-marketing-expert\chrome-extension
5. Click the extension from your Chrome toolbar and test with any business category (e.g. *Bags*, *Cosmetics*, *Hardware*, or *Authors*)!
